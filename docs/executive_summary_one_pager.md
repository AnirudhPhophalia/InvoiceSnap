InvoiceSnap: Executive Summary (One-Page CV Friendly)

Overview
- InvoiceSnap is a scalable, AI-assisted invoice management platform designed to automate and govern the end-to-end lifecycle of business invoices. The system integrates optical data extraction, automated validation, anomaly-aware review, and analytics to deliver high accuracy, operational efficiency, and auditable governance.

Problem and Opportunity
- Manual invoice processing is error-prone and slow, leading to delays and compliance risk. InvoiceSnap targets these challenges by providing a unified ingestion, extraction, validation, and review workflow with actionable insights for finance teams.

Solution Highlights
- Multi-path extraction: digital PDF text parsing, Gemini-based extraction for complex content, and local OCR fallback to maximize coverage.
- Human-in-the-loop: confident extractions are auto-approved while uncertain results prompt reviewer intervention.
- Vendor Intelligence: self-learning Vendor Brain that models vendor behavior, flags anomalies, and explains risk by source.
- Analytics and GST: dashboards, trends, and monthly GST reporting for governance and regulatory compliance.
- Security and Scale: robust authentication, access controls, and a modular architecture designed for SaaS deployment.

Impact and Outcomes
- Reduces manual data entry and processing time, increases data quality, and enables auditable, governance-focused workflows.
- Enables scalable deployment from small teams to enterprise environments with reliable security and governance.

Resume-Ready Takeaway
- Led design and implementation of an AI-powered invoice management platform with end-to-end extraction, anomaly-aware reviews, and GST reporting. Built on Next.js frontend, Node.js/Express backend, and MongoDB, integrating Gemini AI, pdf-parse, and tesseract.js for robust OCR. Delivered a modular, enterprise-grade architecture with a self-learning vendor intelligence module and comprehensive analytics.
