InvoiceSnap: High-Level Overview for Stakeholders and Resume Builders

Overview
- InvoiceSnap is a scalable, AI-assisted invoice management platform designed to streamline the end-to-end lifecycle of invoices for businesses. The solution integrates optical data extraction, automated validation, anomaly-aware review, and comprehensive analytics to deliver accuracy, efficiency, and actionable insights.

- Value Proposition: Reducing manual data entry, accelerating processing times, improving data quality, and enabling better financial governance through auditable workflows and GST reporting.

Target Audience and Impact
- Primary users include finance teams, AP specialists, and executives seeking improved cash flow management and error reduction. The system is designed to scale from small teams to enterprise deployments with secure access controls and robust data governance.
